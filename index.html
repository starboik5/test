Our Story & Heritage

Two Decades of Knitwear Mastery in Tirupur

Established in 2004 in Tirupur—India’s renowned knitwear capital—Win Wins Impex has built a distinguished presence in the global apparel supply chain over the past two decades. With deep-rooted expertise in circular knitwear manufacturing, the company continually evolves into advanced fabrics and contemporary silhouettes.

A proud family-run enterprise, Win Wins Impex is led by visionary Founder & Managing Director Mr. Thiagarajan V, alongside his sons, Managing Partners Mr. Karthikeyan and Mr. Jagath—uniting decades of foundational wisdom with modern manufacturing innovation.

From a modest 8,000 sq. ft. unit to a fully integrated 50,000 sq. ft. manufacturing facility, our journey reflects steady growth, uncompromising quality control, and operational excellence.
